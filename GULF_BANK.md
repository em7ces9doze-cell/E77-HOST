# Gulf Bank — Product plan

All balances, investments, and assets are virtual game mechanics only. No real-money deposits, withdrawals, securities, or financial advice.

2. Wallet balance
3. Daily allowance
4. Work tasks
5. Random bonuses
6. Dice duel against bank (1–10)
7. Player-to-player transfers
8. Transaction history
9. Savings account
10. Savings interest
11. Virtual investment portfolio
12. Virtual market quotes
13. Buy virtual assets
14. Sell virtual assets
15. Daily market movement
16. Investment risk levels
17. Daily challenges
18. Weekly challenges
19. Achievement badges
20. XP and account levels
21. Leaderboard
22. Bank fees and limits
23. Anti-spam cooldowns
24. Per-guild economy settings

Only features implemented and tested in code may be advertised as active.
