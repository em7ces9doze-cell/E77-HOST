# Gulf Bank — Brand System

## Positioning
A premium, Gulf-inspired virtual bank experience inside Discord. This is a fictional entertainment economy, not a real bank or financial service.

## Identity
- Arabic name: بنك الخليج
- English name: GULF BANK
- Brand character: confident, clear, premium, trustworthy, modern.
- Primary colors: Midnight Navy #0B1628, Royal Navy #164B8C, Metallic Gold #C69A4A, Warm Ivory #F4EBDD.
- Typography: clean Arabic sans-serif (Tajawal or IBM Plex Sans Arabic); Inter/Arial for English.
- Mark: Gulf-inspired architectural arch, pearl, and flowing sea lines.
- Avoid: neon glow, casino styling, cluttered gradients, claims of real financial returns.

## Voice
Concise Modern Standard Arabic with familiar Gulf phrasing where appropriate. Every transaction must clearly state amount, outcome, and updated balance.

## Customer experience
The pinned interactive dashboard is the primary entry point. Members use buttons and a select menu rather than repeatedly entering commands. Commands remain available as a fallback.

## Disclosure
All balances, rewards, investments, assets, and games are fictional virtual units (GC) for entertainment only. No real-money deposits, withdrawals, trading, or investment advice.
